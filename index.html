Our Heritage

A CUP WITH A SENSE OF PLACE.

From the misty, high-altitude hills of Chikmagalur—the birthplace of coffee in India—we bring the authentic character of South Indian coffee into every cup.

Roasting arabica and robusta beans in slow batches, our classic filter blend delivers a rich, chocolatey decoction with gentle caramel undertones and zero harsh bitterness.
